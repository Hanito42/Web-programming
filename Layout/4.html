The morning sun rises slowly over the quiet green hills. A cool mist covers the soft grass and whispers through the tall pine trees. The world feels completely still and full of quiet promise.
A small red fox steps softly from the edge of the dark forest. It sniffs the damp air and looks around with bright, curious eyes. Then, it trots quickly toward the bright stream to drink.
The gentle sound of flowing water fills the calm morning air. Soon, little songbirds wake up and begin to sing their sweet daily tunes. A new day has truly and peacefully begun.
